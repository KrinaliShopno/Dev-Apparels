About us

We help with any fire issues & emergencies

Dev Apparels is a leading manufacturer and supplier of high-quality industrial safety wear based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. With manufacturing expertise since 2000, we specialize in producing durable and reliable safety suits, boiler suits, coveralls, and protective workwear for multiple industries.

We focus on delivering premium quality garments made from superior safety fabrics such as fire-resistant materials, cotton, heavy cotton, poly-cotton, and customized fabric combinations as per industry requirements.

Dev Apparels is a trusted manufacturer and supplier of high-quality Industrial Safety Suits and Boiler Suits, designed to provide maximum protection, durability, and comfort in demanding work environments. We cater to industries such as oil & gas, construction, manufacturing, engineering, mining, power plants, and more.
